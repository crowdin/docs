Crowdin offers a range of additional services that can significantly improve your localization workflow while simplifying payment and service management. These services are managed directly through your Crowdin account using a dedicated balance separate from your primary subscription. This means you don't need to register additional accounts to access AI models or translation vendors, such as Crowdin Language Services. By consolidating payments in one place, you gain greater control over your localization resources and expenses, ensuring seamless workflow integration.

## Using Crowdin Credits

Crowdin Credits simplifies the use of paid tools like AI models, translation vendors, and apps by handling payments through a dedicated balance within your Crowdin account. This centralizes balance management and provides a clear overview of your spending.

In the **Crowdin Credits** section, you can:

- **Manage Balance**: Add funds to your dedicated balance for the paid services you use.
- **Set Balance Warnings**: Receive notifications when your balance is low.
- **Track Usage**: View spending with detailed usage statistics.
- **Control App Spending**: Set limits on how much apps can charge to your balance and review their transactions.

To start using Crowdin Credits, [add funds to your account balance](#adding-funds-to-account-balance) and activate the **managed by Crowdin** option for the services you want to use in your projects.

## Managing Your Dedicated Balance

You can manage your Crowdin Credits balance by adding funds and setting up notifications about low balance.

<Image src={balanceManagement} alt="Balance Management" />

### Adding Funds to Account Balance

To add funds to your balance, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Open your **Account Settings** and go to the **Billing** tab.
  2. Locate the **Crowdin Credits** section.
  3. Enter the desired amount in the **Add Funds to Balance** field.
  4. Click **Continue to Payment** to proceed to the checkout page, where you can complete the payment.
</Steps>

<Aside>
  The minimum amount you can add is **$10**.
</Aside>

### Setting Balance Warning Threshold

To ensure uninterrupted operation of paid services, you can set the preferred amount in the **Balance warning threshold** field so that when your balance reaches this point, you'll automatically receive a notification.

To set up low-balance notifications, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Open your **Account Settings** and go to the **Billing** tab.
  2. Locate the **Crowdin Credits** section.
  3. Enter your desired threshold amount in the **Balance warning threshold, $** field.
  4. Click **Save** to confirm your settings.
</Steps>

## Usage Statistics

The **Usage Statistics** section provides a comprehensive visual analysis of your usage costs through an interactive graph, allowing you to review detailed statistics and track spending on each service based on your selected time period. In addition to the graph, the **Used during the selected time period** field shows the total amount spent during the chosen period.

The graph displays usage costs for each service from the [available categories](#available-services). Charges made by apps appear as separate segments of each bar, one for each app, labeled **App:** followed by the app name, and count toward the total. Hover over any bar to see a breakdown of the expenses per service for that day, month, or year.

Depending on the selected date range, each stacked bar represents:

* **One day** for ranges up to **2 months**.
* **One month** for ranges up to **24 months**.
* **One year** for ranges over **24 months**.

You can also focus on specific services by hovering over the service titles under the graph. To hide certain services from the graph, click on their names.

## App Spending Limits

Some apps charge Crowdin Credits for their paid actions. In the **App spending limits** section on the **Billing** tab of your **Account Settings**, you can cap how much apps can charge to your account per day and per month. You can set limits account-wide, as a per-user baseline, and for specific users. When a limit is reached, app charges are declined until the period resets.

For each limit, you can set a **Daily** amount, a **Monthly** amount, or both. Leaving a field empty means no limit for that period. An account-wide limit of $0 stops all app charges, and a default per-user limit of $0 stops them for users without custom limits. By default, account-wide limits are $0, so apps can't charge until you set them. A daily limit can't be higher than the monthly limit. Daily limits reset every day at 00:00 UTC. Monthly limits reset on the 1st of each month at 00:00 UTC.

### Account-Wide Limits

The maximum all apps together can charge across your account. When a limit is reached, app charges are declined for all users until the period resets. Below each field, you can see how much apps have charged in the current period.

### Default Per-User Limits

Applied to every user who doesn't have custom limits. When a user reaches a limit, app charges for their actions are declined until the period resets.

Default per-user limits can't be higher than the account-wide limits.

### Per-User Limits

Give specific users custom limits that replace the default per-user limits, or block app spending for them.

The **Per-user limits** section lists users who have custom limits or have had app charges this month, with the following details:

* **User**: The user the limits apply to.
* **Daily limit**: The daily limit that applies to the user.
* **Monthly limit**: The monthly limit that applies to the user.
* **Daily usage**: How much apps have charged today for the user's actions.
* **Monthly usage**: How much apps have charged this month for the user's actions.

To set custom limits for one or more users, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Click <Icon name="mdi:plus" class="inline-icon" />**Set user limits** in the **Per-user limits** section.
  2. In the **Set user limits** dialog, use the **User** field to select one or more users. Only members of your projects appear in the list. Users who already have custom limits can't be selected.
  3. Choose how to limit the selected users:
     * Enter a **Daily limit**, a **Monthly limit**, or both. Leaving a field empty removes the per-user limit for that period. Leaving both empty means those users have no personal limit (the account-wide limits still apply).
     * Alternatively, enable **Block app spending for this user** to decline all app charges for the selected users, ignoring any limits.
  4. Click **Save**.
</Steps>

To manage the limits of a specific user, click <Icon name="mdi:dots-horizontal" class="inline-icon" /> on the user's row and select one of the following options:

* **Set limits** or **Edit**: Set or change the user's custom limits with **Use custom limits** selected. Clearing it returns the user to the default per-user limits.
* **Block app spending** or **Unblock app spending**: Decline all app charges for the user or lift the block. Unblocking returns the user to the default per-user limits, even if they had custom limits before the block.
* **Remove custom limits**: Return the user to the default per-user limits.

## App Transactions

The **App transactions** section on the **Billing** tab lists the Crowdin Credits charged by apps, newest first, with the following details:

* **Date**: When the app made the charge.
* **App**: The app that made the charge.
* **User**: The user whose action triggered the charge.
* **Amount**: The charged amount.
* **Description**: The app's description of the charge.

To narrow down the list, filter by period (**All time** by default), app (**All apps**), or user (**All users**). To clear the period, click <Icon name="mdi:close" class="inline-icon" />.

To download the transactions, click **Export** and select **Export as CSV** or **Export as JSON**. The export includes the transactions that match the current filters.
