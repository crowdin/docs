Screenshots are a great way to provide translators with additional context for the source strings in your project and, as a result, get more relevant and consistent translations. With screenshots, your team can see which strings are used together on the same page, whether short texts stand for titles, buttons, etc.

Tag source strings to the screenshots to display them to translators in the Editor in the context section below the source string.

<Image src={screenshotsInEditor} alt="Screenshots in Editor" />

## Upload Screenshots

To upload screenshots into your project, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Open your project and select **Screenshots** on the left sidebar.
  1. Drag images from your machine, or click **Upload**.
</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
  To resize high-resolution images to fit your screen, add `@2x` or `@3x` at the end of the filename.
</Aside>

<Image src={screenshotsListInProjectSettings} alt="Screenshots section" />

Next to each screenshot's name, <Icon name="mdi:check-circle" class="inline-icon" /> shows the number of strings tagged on it, and <Icon name="mdi:label" class="inline-icon" /> shows the number of its labels.

## Tag Strings

To show translators where certain strings are located in the UI, tag strings on the screenshots. In Crowdin Enterprise, you can tag strings using the following methods:

* [Auto tag](#tag-strings-automatically)
* [Text recognition](#tag-strings-with-text-recognition)
* [Manual tagging](#tag-strings-manually)

### Tagging Panel and Toolbar

When you open a screenshot for tagging, the panel on the right has two tabs: **Strings** lists the project strings you can tag, and **Labels** holds the screenshot's labels. The number next to each tab shows how many tags or labels the screenshot has.

To give the screenshot more room, click <Icon name="material-symbols:select-window-2-outline" class="inline-icon" /> at the top right of the panel or press <span class="whitespace-nowrap"><KBD>Ctrl</KBD>+<KBD>Shift</KBD>+<KBD>{'\\'}</KBD></span> (or <span class="whitespace-nowrap"><KBD>⌘</KBD>+<KBD>Shift</KBD>+<KBD>{'\\'}</KBD></span>). To open the panel again, select **Strings** or **Labels** at the top right of the screenshot, or press the same keys.

<Image src={screenshotsAutoTag} alt="Tagging panel and toolbar" />

The toolbar at the bottom of the screenshot has the following options:

* <Icon name="mdi:magic-staff" class="inline-icon" /> **Auto tag** (<KBD>A</KBD>): Tag the strings found on the screenshot automatically.
* <Icon name="material-symbols:cleaning-services" class="inline-icon" /> **Clear** (<span class="whitespace-nowrap"><KBD>Shift</KBD>+<KBD>C</KBD></span>): Remove all the tags from the screenshot.
* <Icon name="mdi:cloud-upload" class="inline-icon" /> **Update screenshot**: Upload a new version of the image. This removes all the tags from the screenshot.
* <Icon name="mdi:arrow-all" class="inline-icon" /> **Move the screenshot**: Drag the screenshot to see the rest of a large image. You can also hold <KBD>Ctrl</KBD> (or <KBD>⌘</KBD>) and drag.
* <Icon name="mdi:undo" class="inline-icon" /> **Undo** (<span class="whitespace-nowrap"><KBD>Ctrl</KBD>+<KBD>Z</KBD></span> or <span class="whitespace-nowrap"><KBD>⌘</KBD>+<KBD>Z</KBD></span>) and <Icon name="mdi:redo" class="inline-icon" /> **Redo** (<span class="whitespace-nowrap"><KBD>Ctrl</KBD>+<KBD>Shift</KBD>+<KBD>Z</KBD></span> or <span class="whitespace-nowrap"><KBD>⌘</KBD>+<KBD>Shift</KBD>+<KBD>Z</KBD></span>): Step back or forward through your changes.
* <Icon name="mdi:magnify-plus-outline" class="inline-icon" /> **Zoom in** and <Icon name="mdi:magnify-minus-outline" class="inline-icon" /> **Zoom out**: Switch between the actual size of the screenshot and the size that fits the view. These options appear when the screenshot is larger than the view.

To move the toolbar, drag it by <Icon name="material-symbols:drag-indicator" class="inline-icon" />. Double-click <Icon name="material-symbols:drag-indicator" class="inline-icon" /> to put the toolbar back in place.

Press <KBD>←</KBD> or <KBD>→</KBD> to open the previous or next screenshot, and <KBD>Esc</KBD> to cancel a text selection on the screenshot. The toolbar shortcuts and the arrow keys work when the cursor is outside text fields, such as the search field.

If you open another screenshot or close the window without saving, your changes are kept in your browser, and the screenshot is marked **Unsaved changes** until you save them. To discard them, click <Icon name="mdi:dots-vertical" class="inline-icon" /> (or right-click) on the screenshot and select **Discard changes**.

### Tag Strings Automatically

Crowdin Enterprise detects the text on the screenshot and tags the matching strings from the whole project or from the file, folder, or branch you select.

To tag strings automatically, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Double-click the screenshot, or click <Icon name="mdi:dots-vertical" class="inline-icon" /> (or right-click) on it and select **Edit**.
  1. *(Optional)* To limit the matching to one file, folder, or branch, select it in **Filter strings** in the search settings (click <Icon name="mdi:tune" class="inline-icon" /> to show them).
  1. Click <Icon name="mdi:magic-staff" class="inline-icon" /> in the toolbar or press <KBD>A</KBD>. If the screenshot already has tags, click **Clear tags** to replace them.
  1. Once the strings are added, click **Save**.
</Steps>

To tag strings automatically without opening the screenshots, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Select the screenshots you need. To select several in a row, click the first one, hold <KBD>Shift</KBD>, and click the last one.
  1. Click <Icon name="mdi:magic-staff" class="inline-icon" />.
  1. *(Optional)* To limit the matching to one file, folder, or branch, select it in **Match strings from** in the dialog that opens.
  1. Click **Auto tag**. On screenshots with matching strings, the new tags replace the existing ones. Screenshots without matches keep their tags.
</Steps>

Alternatively, click <Icon name="mdi:dots-vertical" class="inline-icon" /> (or right-click) on the needed screenshot, select **Auto tag**, and follow steps 3 and 4.

If you use In-Context, you can take screenshots of your website pages right from it, and Crowdin Enterprise tags the strings on them automatically.

### Tag Strings with Text Recognition

Select an area with text on the screenshot, and Crowdin Enterprise recognizes the text and looks for matching strings in the project, or only in the file, folder, or branch selected in **Filter strings**. A close match is tagged right away. Otherwise, you can select the best-matching string from the list.

To tag strings with text recognition, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Double-click the screenshot, or click <Icon name="mdi:dots-vertical" class="inline-icon" /> (or right-click) on it and select **Edit**.
  1. Drag over the text on the screenshot to select it.
  1. If the string isn't tagged right away, select it from the list under the selection. To search for different text, edit the recognized text in the field above the list and press <KBD>Enter</KBD>.
  1. Once finished tagging all the strings, click **Save**.
</Steps>

### Tag Strings Manually

To tag strings manually, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Double-click the screenshot, or click <Icon name="mdi:dots-vertical" class="inline-icon" /> (or right-click) on it and select **Edit**.
  1. Find the strings you need with the **Search** field above the list. To narrow the search, use the search settings below the field (click <Icon name="mdi:tune" class="inline-icon" /> to show or hide them):
      * **Filter strings**: Show only the strings from one file, folder, or branch, or only the strings tagged on this screenshot (**Tagged strings**). By default, the filter is set to **All files** (**All branches** in string-based projects).
      * **Search in**: Limit the search to **Source text**, **Source context**, or **Source key**, or widen it to **All fields** (text, context, and key). By default, the search covers **Text and context**. With **Source key**, the list shows the key under each string.
  1. Drag the needed string from the list in the right panel to the corresponding text on the screenshot. Alternatively, click on the strings to tag them in the screenshot without specifying the exact place where they should appear.
  1. *(Optional)* To view additional string details or edit them:
      * Click <Icon name="mdi:information" class="inline-icon" /> at the top right of the panel to show more details (file, key, and context).
      * Hover over a string and click <Icon name="mdi:pencil" class="inline-icon" /> to open the **Edit String** dialog. You can:
        * Update the identifier.
        * Update the source text.
        * Add or update the context.
        * Add or update the labels.
        * Set the **Max. length of the translated text**.
      * Click **Save** after making the changes.
  1. Click **Save** when you finish tagging.
</Steps>

<ReadMore>
  Read more about [String Editing](/enterprise/string-management/#string-editing).
</ReadMore>

To tag all the listed strings at once, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Search or filter the list to show the strings you need.
  1. Click **Tag all**. The strings on the current page of the list are tagged without a specific place on the screenshot. If the list has more pages, go to the next one and click **Tag all** again.
  1. Click **Save**.
</Steps>

### Remove Tags

To remove a tag, click <Icon name="mdi:close" class="inline-icon" /> to the left of it on the screenshot. A tag that you just added with auto tag or text recognition can show <Icon name="mdi:undo" class="inline-icon" /> instead. Click it to reopen the list of matching strings and choose another one, or press <KBD>Esc</KBD> to remove the tag. To remove all the tags of a string, hover over the string in the list and click <Icon name="mdi:close-circle" class="inline-icon" />. To remove all the tags from the screenshot, click **Clear**.

Click **Save** to keep the changes.

## Labels

Use labels in your project for an easy way to organize screenshots by certain topics. Once you add labels to your screenshots, you can filter them by added labels. If you already use labels for your source strings, you can also use the same labels for screenshots.

### Add Labels to Screenshots

You can add labels to one screenshot or to several screenshots at once.

##### Add Labels to One Screenshot

To add labels to one screenshot, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Double-click the screenshot, or click <Icon name="mdi:dots-vertical" class="inline-icon" /> (or right-click) on it and select **Edit**.
  1. In the panel on the right, select **Labels**.
  1. Select the labels you need from the **Labels** drop-down menu. To create a new label, type its name, select **Create new label**, and click **Save** in the **Create label** dialog.
  1. Click **Save**.
</Steps>

##### Add Labels to Multiple Screenshots

To add labels to multiple screenshots at once, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Select the screenshots you need. To select several in a row, click the first one, hold <KBD>Shift</KBD>, and click the last one.
  1. Click <Icon name="mdi:label" class="inline-icon" />.
  1. Select the labels you need in the list. To create a new label, type its name in the search field, select **Create new label**, and click **Save** in the **Create label** dialog. Then select the new label in the list.
  1. Click **Apply**.
</Steps>

## Update Screenshots

You can replace a screenshot with a new image, for example, after the UI changes. Updating the image removes all the tags from the screenshot, so tag the strings again before you save it.

To update a screenshot, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Double-click the screenshot, or click <Icon name="mdi:dots-vertical" class="inline-icon" /> (or right-click) on it and select **Edit**.
  1. Click <Icon name="mdi:cloud-upload" class="inline-icon" /> in the toolbar and select a new image, or drag the image onto the screenshot.
  1. Tag the strings on the new image. For example, click <Icon name="mdi:magic-staff" class="inline-icon" /> to tag them automatically.
  1. Click **Save**.
</Steps>

## Rename Screenshots

To rename a screenshot, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Click <Icon name="mdi:dots-vertical" class="inline-icon" /> (or right-click) on the needed screenshot and select **Rename**.
  1. Specify a new name.
  1. Click **Save**.
</Steps>

## Download Screenshots

You can download screenshots and reuse them in other projects or elsewhere.

To download screenshots, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Select the screenshots you need. To select several in a row, click the first one, hold <KBD>Shift</KBD>, and click the last one.
  1. Click <Icon name="mdi:download" class="inline-icon" />.
</Steps>

Alternatively, click <Icon name="mdi:dots-vertical" class="inline-icon" /> (or right-click) on the needed screenshot and select **Download**.

## Delete Screenshots

To delete a screenshot, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Select the screenshots you need. To select several in a row, click the first one, hold <KBD>Shift</KBD>, and click the last one.
  1. Click <Icon name="mdi:delete" class="inline-icon" />.
  1. Click **Delete** to confirm.
</Steps>

Alternatively, click <Icon name="mdi:dots-vertical" class="inline-icon" /> (or right-click) on the needed screenshot, select **Delete**, and confirm.
