With a style guide, you can create, store, and manage all your project's formatting and tone rules in one place. It keeps voice, style, and terminology consistent across translations, so translators know exactly how your brand should sound in different languages.

Style guides are a native resource, at the same level as [Translation Memory (TM)](/enterprise/translation-memory/) and [Glossary](/enterprise/glossary/). They give human translators a clear reference while they translate, and provide the base instructions for Crowdin AI features like AI Auto-Translation, AI Assistant, and AI QA Checks.

## Creating Style Guides

You can create style guides by writing them from scratch, starting from a ready-made template, or uploading existing MD, PDF, DOCX, or XLSX files. You can then assign them to the relevant projects and target languages.

<Aside type="tip">
  To draft and refine a style guide with AI, use the [Crowdin Copilot app](https://store.crowdin.com/crowdin-copilot).
</Aside>

To create a style guide, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Open your organization's **Workspace** and select **Style Guides** on the left sidebar.
  2. At the bottom right, click <Icon name="mdi:plus" class="inline-icon"/> **Create**.
  3. In the left panel of the appeared dialog, configure the following settings:
      * **Name**: Name your style guide.
      * **Projects**: Select **All in Workspace** (**All in group** for style guides in a group) or **Selected** to assign the style guide to specific projects.
      * **Languages**: *(Optional)* Restrict the style guide to specific target languages. If left empty, it applies to all languages in the assigned projects.
  4. Move to the right side of the dialog and write your style guide content in the built-in Markdown editor. You can also use one of the following options:
      * **Use template**: Fill the editor with a ready-made Markdown template covering brand foundations, voice and tone, mechanics and localization, and inclusivity, and replace the placeholders with your own rules.
      * **Upload file**: Upload an existing `.md`, `.pdf`, `.docx`, or `.xlsx` file. If you upload a Markdown file, its content appears in the built-in editor.
  5. Click **Create**. <Image src={createStyleGuideDialog} alt="Create style guide dialog" />
</Steps>

## Optimizing Style Guides for AI

To use your style guide with AI-powered features (like auto-translation or the AI Assistant), the AI requires a concise summary of your rules to include in its prompts. You manage this summary in the **AI-Ready Version** section of your style guide.

When you create a style guide, Crowdin Enterprise generates the AI-Ready Version from your content automatically. You can also write the summary manually or click **Generate** to have the AI summarize the full guide again.

Crowdin Enterprise tracks whether your AI instructions are up-to-date with your current style guide content. You can view this status in the **AI Version** column on the **Style Guides** page:

* **None**: The style guide does not have an AI-Ready Version and will not be passed to the AI in prompts.
* **Ready**: The AI-Ready Version is fully up-to-date with the current style guide content.
* **Outdated**: The style guide content was modified, but the AI-Ready Version was not regenerated.
  <Aside type="caution">
    If a style guide is **Outdated**, it will still be passed to the AI during translation or QA tasks, but the AI will use the old instructions, which may lead to inaccuracies. Always review and update the AI-Ready Version after editing your style guide.
  </Aside>

## Managing Style Guides

You can manage your style guides directly from the **Style Guides** page on your Workspace home page. This includes searching, filtering, editing, downloading, and deleting them.

### Searching, Filtering, and Sorting Style Guides

By default, all style guides are displayed on the **Style Guides** page. The table displays the style guide's *Name*, *AI Version*, assigned *Projects*, *Languages*, *Owner*, and *Created* date.

To find a specific style guide, use the **Search** field. To filter the list of style guides, click <Icon name="mdi:filter-variant" class="inline-icon" /> **Filter** and use the available options:

* **Projects**: Filter by assigned projects (**All** or a specific project).
* **Languages**: Filter by assigned languages (**All** or a specific language).
* **Owner**: Filter by the user who created the style guide.
* **Created**: Filter by creation date (**All** or **Custom Range**).

To sort style guides, click the **Name** column header once to sort in ascending order and click again to sort in descending order.

To choose which columns to display, click <Icon name="mdi:view-column" class="inline-icon" />.

### Editing Style Guides

To edit a style guide, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Open your organization's **Workspace** and select **Style Guides** on the left sidebar.
  2. Click <Icon name="mdi:dots-vertical" class="inline-icon" /> next to the needed style guide and select **Edit**.
  3. Modify the style guide as needed and click **Save** to save changes.
</Steps>

When editing a style guide, you'll find the **Style guide metadata** section on the left panel. It provides an audit trail of who created or last updated the style guide and when. The section contains the following read-only details:

* **Created by**: The user who created the style guide.
* **Created**: The date and time the style guide was created.
* **Modified by**: The user who last modified the style guide.
* **Last modified**: The date and time the style guide was last modified.

### Downloading Style Guides

You can download your style guides for offline reference and reuse them elsewhere.

To download a style guide, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Open your organization's **Workspace** and select **Style Guides** on the left sidebar.
  2. Click <Icon name="mdi:dots-vertical" class="inline-icon" /> next to the needed style guide and select **Edit**. Alternatively, double-click the needed style guide.
  3. Click <Icon name="mdi:download" class="inline-icon" /> **Download** in the editor toolbar.
</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
  Project members can also download style guides directly from the Editor while working on a project.
</Aside>

### Deleting Style Guides

To delete style guides, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Open your organization's **Workspace** and select **Style Guides** on the left sidebar.
  2. Delete the style guides using one of the following methods:
      * **Single style guide**: Click <Icon name="mdi:dots-vertical" class="inline-icon" /> next to the style guide and select **Delete**.
      * **Multiple style guides**: Select the checkboxes next to the style guides you want to delete, or select the top checkbox to delete all, then click <Icon name="mdi:delete" class="inline-icon" /> at the top of the list.
  3. Confirm the deletion in the confirmation dialog.
</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
  Deleting a style guide permanently removes it and all the rules it contains from your Crowdin Enterprise organization. This action cannot be undone.
</Aside>

## Style Guides in AI QA Checks

When the AI-powered QA check is enabled in your project, it automatically incorporates all assigned style guides into its evaluation prompt. The AI checks that each submitted translation follows the tone, voice, and style rules defined in your style guides.

You don't need to manually select a style guide in your AI prompt settings. Crowdin Enterprise evaluates translations against all the style guides assigned to the project.
