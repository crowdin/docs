You can upgrade or downgrade your subscription plan at any time. Visit the [Pricing page](https://crowdin.com/pricing) to compare available plans and choose the most suitable one.

There are two types of upgrades:

* Within the plan &ndash; the customization by adding managers and hosted words within a subscription plan.
* Between the plans &ndash; the switch to a different subscription plan.

A similar principle is also applicable to downgrades.

## Enabling Advanced Features

Depending on your current subscription plan, some features might not be available. In such cases, Crowdin Enterprise shows a message prompting you to upgrade to a higher plan to use the feature.

<LinkButton href="https://crowdin.com/pricing" icon="external" target="_blank">View Pricing</LinkButton>

For example, if your current subscription plan is Team+, features like SAML, Teams, and IP allowlist will become available once you upgrade to the Business subscription plan or higher.

To see which features your current plan includes, go to **Organization Settings > Billing > Plan Limits & Usage**. The **Plan Limit/Availability** column shows whether each feature is available on your plan.

## What Happens if You Exceed Your Plan Quota

When you exceed your hosted words quota, you will receive an email from us asking to upgrade. You then have 10 more days to upgrade the subscription. Otherwise, your project will be suspended for translators. You can still access your account and project settings, and all data will remain as is until the subscription is updated.

We recommend upgrading the subscription plan beforehand if you expect to have more hosted words uploaded soon.

To see how much of your plan you've used, go to **Organization Settings > Billing > Plan Limits & Usage**.

## Upgrading the Subscription

If your current subscription plan is Team+ and you'd like to upgrade it by adding more managers and hosted words, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  1. Go to **Organization Settings > Billing**.
  2. Click **Change plan**.
  3. Customize the subscription plan according to your needs.
  4. Click **Upgrade**.
</Steps>

To upgrade to the Business or Enterprise subscription plans, [Contact Sales team](https://crowdin.com/contact-sales).
