  <tbody>
  <tr>
    <td>Translate headers and footers</td>
    <td>Headers and footers will be available for translation. Enabled by default.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>Translate hidden text</td>
    <td>The hidden text will be available for translation.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>Translate shape and image names</td>
    <td>The name of a shape or an image will be available for translation. Enabled by default.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>Translate shape and image alternative text</td>
    <td>The alternative text of a shape or an image will be available for translation, which keeps the localized document accessible to screen readers.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>Additional field types to translate</td>
    <td>Defines a list of Word field types whose text will be available for translation. Use the field codes as Word writes them (for example, <code>PAGE</code>). <code>HYPERLINK</code> is translated whether or not it appears in the list.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>Paragraph styles</td>
    <td>Defines a list of paragraph style names, as they appear in the document (for example, <code>Heading1, Quote</code>). Select <strong>Exclude the listed styles</strong> to leave them out, or <strong>Translate only the listed styles</strong> to translate nothing else.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>Highlight colors</td>
    <td>Defines a list of Word highlight colors. Select <strong>Exclude the listed highlight colors</strong> to leave the text they mark out, or <strong>Translate only the listed highlight colors</strong> to translate nothing else. Available colors: <code>black</code>, <code>blue</code>, <code>cyan</code>, <code>green</code>, <code>magenta</code>, <code>red</code>, <code>yellow</code>, <code>white</code>, <code>darkBlue</code>, <code>darkCyan</code>, <code>darkGreen</code>, <code>darkMagenta</code>, <code>darkRed</code>, <code>darkYellow</code>, <code>darkGray</code>, <code>lightGray</code>, <code>none</code>.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>Exclude text in the listed colors</td>
    <td>Text in the listed colors will not be available for translation. Each color is six hexadecimal digits and must match exactly, so a similar shade is still translated.</td>
  </tr>

</table>
